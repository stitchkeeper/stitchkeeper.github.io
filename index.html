Your cross-stitch charts, stitch by stitch.

Open the PDF charts you already own, mark what you have stitched, and always know where you left off. On iPhone and iPad.

  • Your own PDFs. Multi-page charts are joined into one; colors and DMC numbers come from the chart key. Backstitch lines are shown too.
  • Paper charts. Photograph or scan a printed chart, mark its corners, and check the result before you stitch.
  • Fast marking. Tap a stitch, hold and drag along a row, or mark a whole 10×10 square. Apple Pencil marks wherever it goes.
  • Stitcher's tools. Highlight one color, park threads, mark stitches to unpick, keep notes, see your daily stats and a finish estimate.
  • Private. No account, no tracking, no ads. Charts and progress stay on your device.
  • Pay once. One project is free. StitchKeeper Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription.
